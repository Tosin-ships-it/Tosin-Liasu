ABOUT ME

I started with how digital interfaces look. I grew into understanding how products actually work.

I got into UI/UX design because I was curious about digital interfaces and how people interact with them. Working on real products made me realize that the visual layer is only one part of the equation. The bigger questions are what problem we are solving, who we are building for, and how the experience should work.

That pushed me deeper into Product Design, from research and information architecture to user flows, wireframes, UI design, prototyping, and design systems. Working with developers added another perspective. A design does not end in Figma. It becomes a working product, and implementation can change how a solution needs to work.

That is what led me to start learning frontend development and exploring AI-assisted workflows. I want to understand more of the process between an idea, a design, and a working product. I am putting that into practice through personal projects, experimentation, and open-source contributions.

My direction is simple: design, understand, build.

MY PROCESS

Product Design Approach

A practical product-design process focused on understanding the problem and continuing through definition, design, and refinement.

01

UNDERSTAND

I start by understanding the problem, users, context, constraints, and available information before jumping into solutions.

02

DEFINE

I bring structure to what I’ve learned through requirements, user flows, information architecture, and clear design choices.

03

DESIGN

I translate that structure into wireframes, detailed interfaces, prototypes, and reusable design patterns.

04

REFINE

I review the work, test key interactions, collaborate with developers, consider implementation details, and improve the product.
